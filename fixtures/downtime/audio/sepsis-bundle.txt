CHARGE NURSE: Cultures before antibiotics, understood.

CHARGE NURSE: Cefepime 2 grams q8, vancomycin 1500 q12. No pip-tazo.
DR. WHITFIELD: Right. Portable chest X-ray STAT to rule out a pulmonary source. Vital signs every fifteen minutes until he stabilizes. Strict intake and output with hourly urine output — put a catheter in. Keep him NPO.
CHARGE NURSE: Do you want pressors ready?
DR. WHITFIELD: Have norepinephrine at the bedside but do not start it yet — let's see his pressure after the bolus. If the MAP stays under 65 after the full 30 per kilo, start norepinephrine and titrate to a MAP above 65.
DR. WHITFIELD: And call the intensivist now. He needs an ICU bed, not a floor bed.
